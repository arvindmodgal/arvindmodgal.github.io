    Manage firmware¶

    Firmware is the software that runs on a device. Before FirmwareCheck can analyze a product for vulnerabilities, compliance, or zero-day risks, you must upload a firmware image to the corresponding device.

    Firmware image¶

    A firmware image contains the software that runs on a device. Before FirmwareCheck can analyze a product for vulnerabilities, compliance, or zero-day risks, you must upload the firmware image to the corresponding device.

    This section explains how to upload and manage firmware images throughout their lifecycle.

    At a glance¶

    In this section, you'll learn how to:

    • Understand how FirmwareCheck uses firmware images.
    • Upload firmware to a device.
    • Replace an existing firmware image with a newer version.
    • Prepare firmware for security and compliance assessments.

    Typical workflow¶

    To prepare firmware for assessment, complete the following tasks in order:

    1. Upload firmware
    2. Run an assessment
