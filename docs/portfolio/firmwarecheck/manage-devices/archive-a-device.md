# Archive a device

Archive a device when it is no longer actively maintained but you want to keep its firmware, assessment history, and reports for future reference.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to FirmwareCheck.
- Locate the device you want to archive. For more information, see
  [Find a device](find-a-device.md).

## To archive a Device:
1. In the navigation pane, click **FirmwareCheck**.

2. Open the device you want to archive.

3. Click **Archive**.

4. When prompted, confirm that you want to archive the device.

The device is removed from the list of active devices and moved to the archived devices list.

!!! tip

    Archive a device instead of deleting it when you may need to review previous firmware, assessment results, or compliance reports in the future.
