# Delete a device

Delete a device when it is no longer required and its firmware, assessment history, and reports do not need to be retained.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to FirmwareCheck.
- Locate the device you want to delete. For more information, see
  [Find a device](find-a-device.md).

!!! warning

    Deleting a device permanently removes the device and its associated information. This action cannot be undone.

1. In the navigation pane, click **FirmwareCheck**.

1. On the left-hand panel, click **FirmwareCheck**. FirmwareCheck shows all devices with **Active** status.
2. Click the **More options** icon on the device whose status you want to change.
3. Click **Delete device**.

    Device list with the delete device confirmation dialog open.
    ![alt text](../images/delete-device.png)
    *Figure 6: Deleting a device*

4. On the **Delete device** dialog, click **Yes**.

    The device now appears under the **Deleted** tab.
