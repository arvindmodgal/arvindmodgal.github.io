# Device status

Every device in FirmwareCheck has a status that indicates whether it is available for assessments or retained for historical purposes.

Understanding device status helps you choose whether to continue working with a device, archive it for future reference, or remove it from the workspace.

## Device statuses

### Active

Active devices are available for everyday work.

You can:

- Upload firmware.
- Run assessments.
- View reports.
- Update device information.

Use this status for devices that are currently being developed, tested, or maintained.

---

### Archived

Archived devices are retained for historical reference but are hidden from normal day-to-day work.

Archiving a device is useful when a product reaches end of life or is no longer actively maintained, but its assessment history must be preserved.

Archived devices can be restored if they are needed again.

---

### Deleted

Deleted devices are permanently removed from the workspace.

Delete a device only when it is no longer required and its assessment history does not need to be retained.

!!! warning

    Deleting a device permanently removes the device record and its associated information. This action cannot be undone.
