# Troubleshooting

This section describes common issues you may encounter while using FirmwareCheck and suggests steps to resolve them.

## I can't sign in

### Possible causes

- Incorrect email address or password.
- Password has expired.
- Your account has not been activated.
- Your account has been disabled by an administrator.

### Resolution

- Verify that you entered the correct email address and password.
- Reset your password. For more information, see
  [Reset your password](getting-started.md#reset-your-password).
- Contact your administrator if your account has been disabled.

---

## I can't upload firmware

### Possible causes

- The firmware file is corrupted.
- The upload was interrupted.
- The selected file format is not supported.

### Resolution

- Verify that the firmware image is valid.
- Upload the file again.
- Confirm that the firmware image is in a supported format.

---

## My assessment failed

### Possible causes

- The firmware image is incomplete or invalid.
- The assessment service is temporarily unavailable.
- The assessment configuration is incorrect.

### Resolution

- Verify that the uploaded firmware image is complete.
- Run the assessment again.
- Contact your administrator if the problem persists.

---

## I can't connect to Jira

### Possible causes

- The Jira URL is incorrect.
- The API token is invalid.
- Your Jira account does not have permission to create issues.

### Resolution

- Verify the Jira URL.
- Generate a new API token if necessary.
- Confirm that your Jira account has the required permissions.

---

## I can't find a device

### Possible causes

- The device has been archived.
- The device has been deleted.
- Search filters are hiding the device.

### Resolution

- Clear any active filters.
- Check the **Archived** devices list.
- Verify that the device has not been permanently deleted.

---

## Still need help?

If you cannot resolve the issue, contact your FirmwareCheck administrator or your organization's support team.
