# Manage your account

Use your account settings to manage your profile information and password.

## At a glance
This section explains how to:

- Update your profile information.
- Change your password.
- Reset your password if you cannot sign in.

These tasks help ensure that your account details remain accurate and that only you can access your FirmwareCheck account.

## Update your profile

Update your personal information associated with your FirmwareCheck account.

### Before you begin

- Sign in to FirmwareCheck. For instructions, see
  [Sign in to FirmwareCheck](getting-started.md#sign-in-to-firmwarecheck).

### Update your profile

1. Click your user profile in the upper-right corner.
2. Select **Profile**.
3. Update the required information.
4. Click **Save**.

The updated information is available immediately.

## Change your password

Change your password if you know your current password. If you've forgotten it instead, see [Password reset and recovery](#password-reset-and-recovery).

1. Sign in to your FirmwareCheck account.
2. Click the user icon in the top-right corner.
3. Click **Profile**.
4. On the **Profile** dialog, click **Change password**.
3. Type your current password.
5. Type and confirm your new password.
6. Click **Save**.

## Reset your password

If you have forgotten your password and cannot sign in, see
[Reset your password](getting-started.md#reset-your-password).
